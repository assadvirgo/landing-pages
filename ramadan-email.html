Dear PISES Family,

As we welcome the blessed month of Ramadan, we extend our heartfelt greetings to every member of our school community — students, parents, teachers, and staff.

“The month of Ramadan is that in which the Quran was revealed — a guidance for the people and clear proofs of guidance and criterion.”

— Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:185

Ramadan is a time for reflection, compassion, and community. At PISES, we believe these values lie at the heart of education. This month reminds us that learning extends far beyond the classroom — it lives in every act of kindness, patience, and shared humanity.

We pray that this Ramadan brings your family closer together, fills your home with warmth, and renews our collective spirit as a school community.
